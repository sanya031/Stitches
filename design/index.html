Selvedge icons

One line icon set for the stitch playground. Every icon sits on the same 24 px grid with a 1.75 stroke, so they can sit side by side in any control. Click an icon to copy its SVG.

Grid

24 × 24 viewBox, 2 px padding. Content lives inside 2 to 22.

Stroke

1.75 px, round caps and joins, no fills. A solid shape is used only where it means something: a selected square, a pointer, a fabric hole.

Holes

Stitch icons draw the fabric holes as dots, and legs stop just short of them, like thread going in. That is what makes them read as embroidery.

Colour

Icons take the colour of their text (currentColor). On the dark workbench that is linen white; on the active pill it is the dark ink on brass.

Thread icons · stitches and fills

Tune the hover with the Hover zoom panel (top right): the spring for zooming out and back, hover delay, where the hovered stitch anchors (top left by default: it shrinks in place and the rest of the patch spreads out to its right and below), how the others are revealed, patch size, icon sizes and colours. Values are kept in this browser; Copy in the panel gives them to you.

The same stitches and fills drawn as real thread: lens-shaped legs pinched into the holes, round shading, twist ridges, a soft shadow. Fills come from the app's own fill generators. Any thread colour works. Hover one: it shrinks in place and the rest of a 4 × 4 patch, laid as the canvas lays it, spreads out around it. First frame and Last frame show the two ends on their own, on the same grid: the patch's top-left square is exactly the single square at a quarter size, so scaling the patch 4× about the frame's top-left corner of the square (6, 6 on the 48 frame) lines them up. The downloadable *-zoom files still use the earlier centred CSS zoom.

Primitives · stitches and fills

Every thread icon as clean lines on one square: the four holes, then the shape that square has on the canvas, with fewer lines. Stitches use the full stroke; fills a finer one, as they use a finer thread. A gap marks where a thread passes under another. They zoom out the same way, anchored on the square's top-left corner (5, 5 on the 24 grid).

Selvedge icons

One line icon set for the stitch playground. Every icon sits on the same 24 px grid with a 1.75 stroke, so they can sit side by side in any control. Click an icon to copy its SVG.

Grid

24 × 24 viewBox, 2 px padding. Content lives inside 2 to 22.

Stroke

1.75 px, round caps and joins, no fills. A solid shape is used only where it means something: a selected square, a pointer, a fabric hole.

Holes

Stitch icons draw the fabric holes as dots, and legs stop just short of them, like thread going in. That is what makes them read as embroidery.

Colour

Icons take the colour of their text (currentColor). On the dark workbench that is linen white; on the active pill it is the dark ink on brass.

Thread icons · stitches and fills

The same stitches and fills drawn as real thread: lens-shaped legs pinched into the holes, round shading, twist ridges, a soft shadow. Fills come from the app's own fill generators. Any thread colour works.

Hover one: it shrinks in place and the rest of a patch of the same stitch spreads out to its right and below, laid the way it is stitched. The patch has fewer squares at smaller sizes so you can still read it. Tune how fast and how smooth with the Hover zoom panel (top right). First frame and Last frame show the two ends on their own: the patch's top-left square is exactly the single square, scaled down. The downloadable *-zoom files still use the earlier centred CSS zoom.

Primitives · stitches and fills

Every thread icon as clean lines on one square: the four holes (faint, they are the cloth), then the stitch. Stitches use the full stroke; fills a finer one, as they use a finer thread. A gap marks where a thread passes under another. The patch is the same drawing with more squares: laid and woven fills run on as one thread, counted and scattered stitches repeat square by square.

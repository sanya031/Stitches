Selvedge icons

The icons for the stitch playground: line icons for the interface, and stitch and fill icons as thread and as clean primitives. Line icons share one 24 px grid, one 1.75 stroke and one size, and move on hover. Click an icon to copy its SVG.

Grid

24 × 24 viewBox. Ink stays in the 3 to 21 live area. A square icon spans 4 to 20, a round one is r 8.5, so every icon reads as the same size.

Stroke only

One 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins. No fills, no second tone, no opacity. A dot is a zero-length stroke, the stroke's own round cap.

Motion

Hover an icon (or the control it sits in) and the part that does the work moves: the needle dips, the marquee marches, the hand grabs, the eraser rubs. Each move ends where it started; reduced motion keeps them still.

Colour

Icons take the colour of their text (currentColor). On the dark workbench that is linen white; on the active pill it is the dark ink on brass.

Colour · tonal palettes

Every palette shares one lightness per step, set by the primary at 500, so any two steps have about the same contrast whatever the hue. Components will use roles (panel, text, border, accent…) that point at these steps, with a light and a dark value; the raw steps live in tokens.css. Click a swatch to copy its hex.

Colour · roles

What components use. Each role points at a palette step, with a light and a dark value. The specimen is only a way to see every role in place; it is not the panel design. Numbers on the specimen match the list. To try a change, pick another step: the specimen and the contrast update live, for the theme shown. Copy the changes as CSS when you are happy, and they go into tokens.css.

Controls · stage sliders

For settings a newcomer may not know. Drag a slider (or use the arrow keys): like the brush-size preview in Procreate, a bubble rides the thumb with one square of cloth and the thread on it, redrawn as you go. More strands thicken the thread. A higher count shrinks the square while the thread stays the same width. Density packs the lines closer. Let go and it settles on a real value and fades.

Thread icons · stitches and fills

The same stitches and fills drawn as real thread: lens-shaped legs pinched into the holes, round shading, twist ridges, a soft shadow. Fills come from the app's own fill generators. Any thread colour works.

Hover one: it shrinks in place and the rest of a patch of the same stitch spreads out to its right and below, laid the way it is stitched. The patch has fewer squares at smaller sizes so you can still read it. Tune how fast and how smooth with the Hover zoom panel (top right). First frame and Last frame show the two ends on their own: the patch's top-left square is exactly the single square, scaled down. The downloadable *-zoom files carry the same zoom as CSS, at the tuned duration and smoothness (in icons/zoom-css.js).

Primitives · stitches and fills

Every thread icon as clean lines on one square: the four holes (faint, they are the cloth), then the stitch. Stitches use the full stroke; fills a finer one, as they use a finer thread. A gap marks where a thread passes under another. The patch is the same drawing with more squares: laid and woven fills run on as one thread, counted and scattered stitches repeat square by square.
